# NFL adapter

Facts about ESPN's NFL data that the code depends on. Loaded only when working in this folder; the root CLAUDE.md has the rules (validation policy, fixtures first, CACHE_VERSION). The source, hosts and response shapes are the NBA's (`src/adapters/nba/CLAUDE.md`), with football's stats: read that file too.

ESPN's site API: unofficial and undocumented, no key, and **reachable from Claude sessions**, so fixtures are recorded here with curl.

## Which endpoint has what

- `site.api.espn.com/apis/site/v2/sports/football/nfl/scoreboard?dates=YYYYMMDD`: the day's games (US Eastern date: a game at 8:20 pm ET is on that day, though its UTC time is the next). **Without `dates` it returns the whole week** (Thursday to Monday), with `week.number`. Per event: `status.type.state` (`pre`, `in`, `post`) and `name`, `period` (1-4, 5 is overtime, 0 before kickoff) and `displayClock`; competitors with `score` as a string, `records` (`type: "total"` is the season record, "6-5" or "6-4-1" with a tie) and `linescores` (`{ value, period }` per quarter played). Competitors have **no `leaders`** (the NBA's do): the game's passing, rushing and receiving leaders are on `competitions[0].leaders` (`name` "passingYards", a `displayValue` line "17/29, 198 YDS, 1 TD, 2 INT", the athlete's `shortName`, and the team as ESPN's numeric id, not the abbreviation). `competitions[0].situation` (live) becomes `Game.situation`: `downDistanceText`, the team with the ball as ESPN's numeric team id (`possession`, matched to the competitor's `team.id`), `isRedZone`; it is kept only while the status is `STATUS_IN_PROGRESS` and `down` is 1-4. The game summary has no `situation` (only a `possession` flag per header competitor, and drives whose plays include timeouts and kickoffs), so the game page does not show it. Not used yet: those leaders, `notes`, `broadcasts`.
- `site.api.espn.com/apis/v2/sports/football/nfl/standings?level=3`: two conferences ("American Football Conference", "National Football Conference"), each four divisions ("AFC East") of four teams. Entries are **not sorted**: `playoffSeed` is the conference seed (1-16, the league's tiebreakers; 1-4 are the division leaders), which orders each division and becomes `conferenceRank`; `CONFERENCE_BANDS` marks seeds 1-7 as the playoffs. Stats by `name`: `wins`, `losses`, `ties`, `winPercent` (a tie counts as half a win), `streak` ("W6"), `Home` and `Road` ("4-2", "2-1-1" with a tie), `vs. Div.`, `vs. Conf.`, `pointsFor`, `pointsAgainst`, `differential`. `gamesBehind` is behind the **division** leader, unlike the NBA's (conference), so it is not mapped. Points for and against are not mapped yet (the domain's `goalsFor` is hockey).
- `site.web.api.espn.com/apis/common/v3/sports/football/nfl/statistics/byathlete?...&isqualified=true&limit=N&sort=<group>.<stat>:desc`: the top N players by one stat, one request per board (`LEADER_CATEGORIES`). Groups: `general`, `passing`, `rushing`, `receiving`, `defensive`, `defensiveinterceptions`, `scoring`, `returning`, `kicking`, `punting`, each with its `names`. **The sort key's group is case-sensitive and differs from the response's name**: `defensiveInterceptions.interceptions` sorts (lower case answers 400), and the response calls the group `defensiveinterceptions`; the mapper matches groups ignoring case. Values are season totals (sacks in halves, passer rating with many decimals). Qualified players only: about 53 passers, 59 rushers, 175 receivers.
- `site.api.espn.com/apis/site/v2/sports/football/nfl/summary?event=<id>` (game page): the NBA's shape. The header's `status` has `period` and `displayClock` only while live (a final's has only `type`: the mapper counts `linescores`, one per quarter started, `displayValue` "6"); a competitor's `score` is absent before kickoff, and `record` (`type: "total"`) is used only then. `boxscore.players[]` holds **ten groups per team** by `name` (passing, rushing, receiving, fumbles, defensive, interceptions, kickReturns, puntReturns, kicking, punting), each with `keys` ("completions/passingAttempts"), `labels` ("C/ATT") and athletes' `stats` as strings ("17/29", "--" for none); **no starter flag and no position** there; absent before kickoff. `boxscore.teams` is 25 team stats by `name` with a `label` (firstDowns, thirdDownEff "2-8", totalYards, possessionTime "19:20"; `TEAM_GAME_STATS` picks them); **before kickoff it holds 8 season averages** (yardsPerGame), so the mapper reads it only live or final. `leaders[]` per team (`team.abbreviation`): categories by `name` ("passingYards", "rushingYards", "receivingYards", "sacks", "totalTackles"), each leader with a `displayValue` line ("32 CAR, 184 YDS, 1 TD"), `value` (the yards) and `athlete` (with `position` and `headshot`); before kickoff they are season leaders, not read. `scoringPlays[]`: `text` ("Kyren Williams 7 Yd Rush (Brandon McManus Kick)", sometimes with a trailing space), `scoringType.displayName` ("Touchdown", "Field Goal"), `awayScore` and `homeScore` after it, `period.number`, `clock.displayValue` (time left, "11:29"), `team.abbreviation`; empty before the first score. `gameInfo`: `venue`, `attendance` (on the final; absent live), `weather`, `officials`. Not read: `drives`, `plays`, `winprobability`, `injuries`, `odds`, `news`. About 600 KB final.
- `site.api.espn.com/apis/site/v2/sports/football/nfl/teams/<abbrev>/athletes/statistics` (team page stats, about 650 KB): `results[]` by category `name` ("passing", "rushing", "receiving", "defensive", "kicking", "punting", "returning", "scoring"), each with `leaders[]`, the players with stats there in ESPN's order (by the category's main stat; 45 on defence). Each player's stats come in groups by `name` (the category's own, "general" with `gamesPlayed`, and for defence **`defensiveInterceptions`**, where interceptions are), each a list by stat `name` with `displayValue` as ESPN shows it ("2,763"). `TEAM_STAT_TABLES` picks the columns; returning and scoring are not shown. Unlike the NBA's (one list by points), a player appears once per category.
- `site.api.espn.com/apis/site/v2/sports/football/nfl/teams/<abbrev>/schedule` (team page games, an extra): the 17 regular-season games with `week` ("Week 12") and the team's `byeWeek`; per event the status is on `competitions[0].status` and scores are `{ value, displayValue }`, as the NBA's. A "home" game can be abroad (the Rams' week 1 at the Melbourne Cricket Ground), so the stadium is the venue most home games use.
- `site.web.api.espn.com/apis/common/v3/sports/football/nfl/athletes/<id>?region=us&lang=en` (player page): the NBA's bio fields (`displayDOB` month first with `region=us`, `displayHeight` `6' 3"`, `displayWeight` "214 lbs", `displayBirthPlace` "Dallas, TX", `displayDraft` "2009: Rd 1, Pk 1 (DET)", absent for an undrafted player), `position.name` ("Quarterback", "Place kicker", turned into sentence case and Canadian spelling), `jersey`, `team`, and `statsSummary` with the position's headline stats ("2026 regular season stats": YDS, TD, INT, QBR for a quarterback; SOLO, FF, INT, PD for a safety).
- `.../athletes/<id>/stats?region=us&lang=en[&seasontype=3]`: a category per kind of stat, **the player's main one first** ("passing" for a quarterback; a safety has only "defensive"; a kicker "kicking", then "punting"...), each with `names` (with `gamesPlayed`), one line per season and team (`season.displayName` "2026", `teamSlug`; oldest first) and the career line in `totals`. `PLAYER_COLUMNS` picks the columns per category; the page shows only the main one. Kicking stats come paired ("fieldGoalsMade-fieldGoalAttempts" "9-9"). The playoffs work as the NBA's (`seasontype=3`, kept only if its filter says "3"). A `league` filter offers "college-football" too, not asked for.
- `.../athletes/<id>/gamelog?region=us&lang=en`: this season's games, the NBA's shape, with the position's `names` (no `gamesPlayed`; a quarterback's passing then rushing; a kicker's "extraPointsMade-extraPointAttempts" in one column); "-" for a stat that does not apply.
- Team ids are ESPN's abbreviations (`BUF`, `LAR`, `WSH`, `JAX`), the same in every endpoint.

## Fixtures

Recorded 2026-11-26 about 8:50 am ET (the morning of Thanksgiving: GB at LAR, final the night before), and Thanksgiving's scoreboard at 1:18 pm ET (CHI at DET live, DET 3-0 with 8:31 left in Q1; PHI at DAL and KC at BUF scheduled):

```sh
cd src/adapters/nfl/__fixtures__
B=https://site.api.espn.com/apis/site/v2/sports/football/nfl
curl -s "$B/scoreboard?dates=20261125" -o scoreboard-2026-11-25.json   # GB 13 at LAR 26, final
curl -s "$B/scoreboard?dates=20261126" -o scoreboard-2026-11-26.json   # at 1:18 pm ET: CHI at DET live, 2 scheduled
curl -s "https://site.api.espn.com/apis/v2/sports/football/nfl/standings?level=3" -o standings-now.json
# Game summaries, at 1:53 pm ET:
curl -s "$B/summary?event=401873079" -o summary-401873079-final.json   # GB 13 at LAR 26
curl -s "$B/summary?event=401873081" -o summary-401873081-live.json    # CHI 3 at DET 10, 10:52 left in Q2
curl -s "$B/summary?event=401873082" -o summary-401873082-pre.json     # KC at BUF, scheduled
# The Rams after 11 games, 2026-11-27 just after midnight ET:
curl -s "$B/teams/LAR/athletes/statistics" -o team-LAR-athletes-statistics.json
curl -s "$B/teams/LAR/schedule" -o team-LAR-schedule.json
# Players, the same night: Stafford (QB, 18 seasons, playoffs), Quentin Lake (S), Brandon McManus (K, undrafted).
A=https://site.web.api.espn.com/apis/common/v3/sports/football/nfl/athletes; Q="region=us&lang=en"
for id in 12483 4249128 16339; do
  curl -s "$A/$id?$Q" -o athlete-$id.json
  curl -s "$A/$id/stats?$Q" -o athlete-$id-stats.json
  curl -s "$A/$id/gamelog?$Q" -o athlete-$id-gamelog.json
done
curl -s "$A/12483/stats?$Q&seasontype=3" -o athlete-12483-stats-playoffs.json   # Lake's and McManus's are not recorded
L="https://site.web.api.espn.com/apis/common/v3/sports/football/nfl/statistics/byathlete?region=us&lang=en&contentorigin=espn&isqualified=true&page=1&limit=20"
for s in passing.passingYards passing.passingTouchdowns passing.QBRating rushing.rushingYards rushing.rushingTouchdowns \
  receiving.receivingYards receiving.receptions receiving.receivingTouchdowns defensive.sacks defensive.totalTackles \
  defensiveInterceptions.interceptions; do curl -s "$L&sort=$s:desc" -o leaders-$s.json; done
```

Trimmed after recording, keeping everything the adapter reads: scoreboards lose `links`, `headlines`, `tickets`, `odds`, `highlights`, `broadcasts` and `geoBroadcasts` at every level; standings lose `links` and `seasons`. A summary keeps only `header` (its `id`, `competitions`, `week`, `season`), `boxscore`, `leaders`, `scoringPlays` and `gameInfo` (only `venue`, without `images`, and `attendance`); everywhere in it `links`, `uid`, `guid`, `descriptions` and logo details (`rel`, `alt`, `lastUpdated`) are removed, and `logos` cut to the first. The team stats keep only `results` (`name`, and each player's `athlete` (`id`, `displayName`, `headshot.href`, `position` `abbreviation` and `name`) and `statistics` (each group's `name` and its stats' `name` and `displayValue`)); the schedule keeps `events`, `byeWeek` and `team`, without `links`, `uid`, `guid`, `tickets`, `broadcasts`, `leaders`, `notes`, `odds`, `headlines` or logo details. An athlete keeps only the fields in `RawAthleteSchema` (and `fullName`, `status`, `displayExperience`), its team cut to its ids, names and first logo; stats lose `glossary`, each category its `displayNames`, `labels` and `descriptions`, and `teams` keep only `shortDisplayName`; a game log loses `glossary`, and its events keep only `id`, `atVs`, `gameDate`, `week`, and the opponent's and team's abbreviations. A leaders file keeps only the board's own group, in `categories` (`name` and `names`) and in each athlete's (`name` and `values`); athletes lose `links`, `teams`, ids other than `id`, `status`, `age` and `slug`, and `teamLogos` is cut to its first.
